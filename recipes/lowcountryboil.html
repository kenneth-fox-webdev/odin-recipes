Low Country Boil

A traditional Southern dish made with shrimp, sausage, corn, and potatoes.

Ingredients

  • Shrimp
  • Sausage
  • Corn
  • Potatoes

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
  2. Add the shrimp and cook until they turn pink.
  3. Add the sausage, corn, and potatoes.
  4. Cook for 10-15 minutes or until the vegetables are tender.
