Lasagna

A classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Ground beef

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Layer the pasta, sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  3. Bake for 25 minutes.
  4. Let it cool before serving.
