Pizza

A classic Italian dish made with a crispy crust, tomato sauce, and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Pepperoni

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough and place it on a baking sheet.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Top with shredded cheese and pepperoni slices.
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
